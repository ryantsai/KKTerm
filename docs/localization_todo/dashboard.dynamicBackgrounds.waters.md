# dashboard.dynamicBackgrounds.waters

- **English value**: `Waters`
- **Namespace**: `app`
- **File/component**: `src/modules/dashboard/registry/watersBackground.tsx`
- **UI role**: `label`
- **User flow**: Shown as the name of a dynamic background option in the Dashboard background picker (Dynamic tab) and in the live background preview dialog, alongside `Ocean` and `Misty Sea`.
- **Tone**: `concise/neutral, one or two words`
- **Placeholders**: `none`
- **Context/meaning**: "Waters" names an open, sunlit tropical sea seen from just above the surface — the expanse of water itself, not a body of water with a name, not a beach or coast, and not drinking water. It must stay distinguishable from the sibling options `ocean` ("Ocean") and `mistySea` ("Misty Sea"), which are separate keys with their own scenes.
- **Domain notes**: This is a Dashboard dynamic background name, not a Module or a setting. Locales that lack a natural plural for "waters" should choose a short poetic term for an open water expanse rather than reusing their translation of "Ocean" or "Sea".
